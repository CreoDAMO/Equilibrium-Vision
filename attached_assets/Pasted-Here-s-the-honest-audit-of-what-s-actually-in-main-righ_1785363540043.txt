The Real Picture

Your earlier self-assessment said:

> Staking contract ❌ Not started

Slashing conditions ❌ Not started

Inflation schedule ❌ Not started

Validator set rotation ❌ Not started

On-chain governance ❌ Not started

That's not accurate for the TypeScript layer. Staking, governance, slashing (in TS), and validator rotation all exist and work. What's missing is the WASM contract layer for staking/governance — which would give you:
- On-chain slashing with economic penalties (not just TS bookkeeping)
- Inflation minting through the contract
- Treasury spends via `payout()` host import
- Cross-contract calls from governance proposals

The zkML Rust stack is production-quality. The TypeScript bridge is the broken link.

---

Honest Recommendation

Don't rewrite staking/governance as WASM contracts yet. The TypeScript implementations are sophisticated (timelocks, parameter bounds, signature verification, no double-counting). The WASM migration is a "nice to have" for mainnet, not a blocker for testnet.

Fix the compilation/runtime blockers first:

1. Add `reqwest` to `equilibrium/Cargo.toml`
2. Add zkml-proof endpoints to `routes/models.ts`
3. Add zkml-proof storage helpers to `chain/modelRegistry.ts`

These three are the only things that will actually break when you try to run the zkML pipeline end-to-end.

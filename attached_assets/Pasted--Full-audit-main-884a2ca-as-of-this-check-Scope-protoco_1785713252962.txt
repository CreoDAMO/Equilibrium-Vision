## C. OPEN or weaker than the map claimed

### C1. Bridge “aggregate BLS” — **do not treat as closed on tip**
On **current `main`**, `contracts/cross_chain_relay/src/lib.rs` still documents / structures **Ed25519 multi-sig** (`sig` 64 bytes, `pubkey` 32 bytes) in `submit_inbound`.  

`0a4ecae` introduced BLS; tip commit `884a2ca` (“Refactor cross-chain relay…”) may have moved the surface again.  

**Audit finding:** Before any BLS/aggregate claim, re-read tip contract + TS route and confirm which interface is live. Map status should be **PARTIAL / verify tip**, not “BLS landed for good.”

### C2. A7 zero-seed DHT
Kad + bootstrap list + QR only. **OPEN** as stated.

### C3. zkML
Module comments exist; not a model-inference circuit. **OPEN.**

### C4. Field proof
No substitute for two-device mesh on hardware. **OPEN as product proof.**

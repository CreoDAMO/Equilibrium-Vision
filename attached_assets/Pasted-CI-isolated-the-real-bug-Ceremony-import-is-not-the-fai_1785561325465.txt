## Why self-test fails

Typical broken pattern (matches your earlier debugging):

| Method | Broken behavior |
|--------|-----------------|
| `instance_map_with_evaluation` | Delegates to **LibsnarkReduction** (public-input padding in A) |

Setup and prove must use the **same** QAP rules. Mixed conventions ⇒ `verify == false` even on fresh keys.

## 1. “Full residual / Lagrangian inside ZK”

**What is there**

`StationarityCircuit` is real R1CS:

- Public: `residual_fp`, `threshold_fp`, `block_hash_lo/hi`
- Witness: `difference`
- Constraints: `residual + difference = threshold`, `difference ≠ 0` (via inverse), difference in 64 bits

Groth16 prove/verify path is live. RISC Zero **stationarity guest** proves the same identity. `StationarityProof::prove/verify` wires into `consensus::validate_block`.

**What is not there**

No constraint that re-runs `joint_residual_and_gradient` / SHA / Lagrangian. Public residual is **trusted as an input**; the proof only shows it sits below threshold via a positive difference.

**Verdict:** Cryptographic proof of **threshold inequality**, not of **solver correctness**. My earlier “simplified circuit” label stands; the *implementation* of that simplified claim is complete.
